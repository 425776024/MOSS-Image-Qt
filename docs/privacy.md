# 新身数字人 / XAvatar Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-22 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

## 中文

本政策适用于王兆蕾（Zhaolei Wang）提供的 macOS 公开发行版“新身数字人”（英文商店名 XAvatar by FFClip，应用内名称 XAvatar）。隐私咨询邮箱：xinshen@ffclip.com。

### 本机创作与存储

应用不要求注册账号。输入的文字、导入的照片、人脸图像、语音和视频，以及生成的作品，在你的 Mac 上处理。公开发行版不向开发者的服务器上传这些创作内容，也不提供远程推理服务。人脸关键点和语音参考用于你发起的数字人和语音创作，不用于识别你的身份。

作品、设置、模型和运行日志保存在本机。你可以删除不再需要的作品和文件；导出到其他文件夹的副本需要在相应位置另行删除。系统备份、云盘同步以及你主动分享或导出的文件由你选择的服务处理。

### 模型下载与外部服务

App Store 版本默认从 Apple 托管的资源包下载模型。Windows / GitHub / DMG 版本的模型仅从 ModelScope 下载。模型下载会向所选服务发送模型文件请求；服务提供方可以接收 IP 地址和完成请求所需的网络信息，但应用不会为下载模型而发送你的创作提示词、照片或语音。

Apple 和其他下载服务按照各自的隐私政策处理其服务数据。应用不含广告追踪或开发者使用情况分析功能。macOS、App Store 的诊断和分析设置由 Apple 管理。

### 联系我们

如果你主动发送客服邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及你提供的内容，用于处理咨询。请勿发送不必要的个人信息；分享日志或作品前请先检查其中的内容。关于客服资料访问、更正或删除的请求，请发送至 xinshen@ffclip.com。

本应用面向 18 岁及以上用户。请仅导入你有权使用的素材，涉及他人人脸或声音时应取得相应授权。政策更新将发布在本页面。

## English

This policy applies to the public macOS releases of 新身数字人, distributed by Zhaolei Wang under the English store name XAvatar by FFClip and the in-app name XAvatar. Contact: xinshen@ffclip.com.

### Local creation and storage

No app account is required. Text prompts, imported photographs, face images, audio and video, and generated works are processed on your Mac. Public releases do not upload this creative content to the developer's servers or offer remote inference. Facial landmarks and reference voices support the avatar and speech tasks you initiate; they are not used to identify you.

Projects, preferences, models and operational logs are stored locally. You can delete unwanted projects and files. Copies exported elsewhere must be deleted separately at their destination. System backups, cloud-folder synchronization and files you choose to share or export are handled by the services you select.

### Model downloads and external services

The App Store version downloads Apple-hosted model asset packs by default. Windows / GitHub / DMG releases download models only from ModelScope. Downloading a model sends file requests to the selected provider, which can receive your IP address and network information needed to service those requests. The app does not send your creative prompts, photographs or voices as part of a model download.

Apple and other download providers handle their service data under their respective privacy policies. The app includes no advertising tracking or developer usage analytics. Apple manages macOS and App Store diagnostic and analytics settings.

### Contact

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include to respond to your request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information, and review logs or creative files before sharing them. Contact xinshen@ffclip.com to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence.

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. Import only material you have the right to use, and obtain appropriate permission when using another person's face or voice. Policy updates will be published on this page.
